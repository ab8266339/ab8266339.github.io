Biography

Dr. Hao Xu is a leading researcher in DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks) and an Assistant Professor at the College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Tongji University. As a pioneering expert in bridging blockchain technology with physical infrastructure, Dr. Xu's research focuses on enabling decentralized wireless networks, IoT systems, and next-generation Web3 infrastructure.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Glasgow, United Kingdom, in 2022. Prior to joining Tongji University, Dr. Xu served as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Glasgow, a Long-term Visiting Researcher at China Mobile Research Institute, and a Senior Engineer at Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd., where he gained extensive industry experience in wireless communications and network infrastructure.

Research Interests

Dr. Xu's research is at the forefront of DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks), exploring how blockchain and decentralized technologies can revolutionize physical infrastructure systems:

  • DePIN & Decentralized Infrastructure: Designing and optimizing decentralized physical infrastructure networks for wireless systems, IoT devices, and edge computing
  • Blockchain Consensus Mechanisms: Developing efficient consensus protocols for resource-constrained devices and wireless blockchain networks
  • Wireless Blockchain Networks: Integrating blockchain technology with 5G/6G networks, creating trustless communication infrastructure
  • Decentralized IoT Systems: Building secure, scalable decentralized architectures for Internet of Things applications
  • Web3 Infrastructure: Advancing the technical foundations of Web3 physical layer networks
  • Data Privacy & Network Security: Ensuring secure and private data transmission in decentralized systems
