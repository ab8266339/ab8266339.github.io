Biography

Dr. Hao Xu is an Assistant Professor and Master's Supervisor at the College of Electronics and Information Engineering, Tongji University. He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Glasgow, United Kingdom, in 2022. Prior to joining Tongji University, Dr. Xu served as a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Glasgow, a Long-term Visiting Researcher at China Mobile Research Institute, and a Senior Engineer at Huawei Technologies Co., Ltd.

Honors and Awards

In 2024, Dr. Xu was selected for multiple prestigious talent programs, including:

  • Shanghai Overseas High-level Talent Program (Youth Category)
  • Shanghai Pujiang Talent Program
  • Qiming Star-Yangfan Special Program

Research Interests

Dr. Xu's research focuses on:

  • Blockchain consensus mechanisms
  • Decentralized network architectures
  • Wireless communications
  • Data privacy and security

Publications and Impact

Since 2020, Dr. Xu has published over 30 peer-reviewed papers, including 10 articles in SCI-indexed journals. His work has garnered more than 1,500 citations on Google Scholar, with citations appearing in high-impact journals such as The Lancet and Nature. Notably, his research includes:

  • Two ESI Highly Cited Papers (441 citations and 251 citations, respectively)
  • One additional highly cited paper (229 citations)
  • Three papers with over 100 citations each

Patents, Standards, and Book Chapters

Dr. Xu holds 10 invention patents (including 1 UK patent) and has contributed to 5 ETSI/O-RAN international standards. He has also authored 3 academic book chapters in his field.

Research Projects

Dr. Xu has participated in 2 EPSRC-funded projects on blockchain and Internet of Things (IoT). He currently serves as Co-Principal Investigator (Co-PI) on an industry-collaborative research project.
